Yes — we've taught Qur'an and Islamic studies online since 2015, with real, published testimonials from students and parents across the US, UK, Canada and beyond. You're welcome to sit in on your first class and ask your tutor about their background.

Will scheduling really work with my time zone?

Yes. We schedule classes 24/7, so wherever you're based, we'll find a consistent weekly time that fits your routine — not the other way around.

How do I start Quran, Islamic studies, and Arabic learning classes online?

You can start learning the Quran from any part of the world and at any time with real-life teachers from any smart device with internet access. The web-based meeting and audio-video software enable students to learn the Quran and Arabic online from their homes.

Who will be the tutor of my Quran course?

According to your needs and requirements, BAYTUL HIDAYA's management will schedule your classes with the best teacher.

How long will it take to learn the Quran, Arabic, or Islamic studies?

It will take approximately 10 to 12 weeks for a whole Tajweed course. Memorizing the complete Quran varies from student to student but approximately (on average) it will take 12 to 24 months. Arabic language courses can last anywhere from 6 to 12 months and for Islamic studies, one grade will take 5 to 10 weeks.
