About Us

Learn more about BAYTUL HIDAYA and our mission.

Welcome to BAYTUL HIDAYA

BAYTUL HIDAYA is a leading online Quran academy dedicated to providing high-quality Islamic education to students around the world. With over 8 years of experience in teaching Quran and Islamic studies, we have established ourselves as a trusted institution for families seeking authentic Islamic education.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make Quranic education accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or schedule. We believe that every Muslim should have the opportunity to learn the Quran with proper tajweed and understanding, guided by qualified and experienced teachers.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every Muslim has access to quality Islamic education, enabling them to connect with the Quran and strengthen their faith. Through our online platform, we aim to bridge the gap between traditional Islamic education and modern technology.

Our Story

How BAYTUL HIDAYA began its journey

BAYTUL HIDAYA was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to make Quranic education accessible to Muslims around the world. Recognizing the challenges that many families face in finding qualified Quran teachers in their local areas, we set out to create an online platform that connects students with experienced Islamic scholars from the comfort of their homes.

Starting with just a handful of students and teachers, we have grown into a thriving online academy with students from across the globe. Our commitment to quality education, personalized attention, and flexible scheduling has made us a preferred choice for families seeking authentic Islamic education.

Today, we continue to expand our offerings and improve our teaching methods, always staying true to our core values of excellence, integrity, and dedication to serving the Muslim community.
