Is BAYTUL HIDAYA legitimate?

Yes — we've taught Qur'an and Islamic studies online since 2015, with real, published testimonials from students and parents across the US, UK, Canada and beyond. You're welcome to sit in on your first class and ask your tutor about their background.

Will scheduling really work with my time zone?

Yes. We schedule classes 24/7, so wherever you're based, we'll find a consistent weekly time that fits your routine — not the other way around.

How do I start Quran, Islamic studies, and Arabic learning classes online?

You can start learning the Quran from any part of the world and at any time with real-life teachers from any smart device with internet access. The web-based meeting and audio-video software enable students to learn the Quran and Arabic online from their homes.

Who will be the tutor of my Quran course?

According to your needs and requirements, BAYTUL HIDAYA's management will schedule your classes with the best teacher.

How long will it take to learn the Quran, Arabic, or Islamic studies?

It will take approximately 10 to 12 weeks for a whole Tajweed course. Memorizing the complete Quran varies from student to student but approximately (on average) it will take 12 to 24 months. Arabic language courses can last anywhere from 6 to 12 months and for Islamic studies, one grade will take 5 to 10 weeks.

What is the cost of courses?

Our courses are competitively priced starting from $20-70 per month for basic courses. Advanced courses like Quran memorization are priced at $100-150 per month. We also offer family discounts and special packages for multiple course enrollments.

Do you offer female teachers for female students?

Yes, we have qualified female teachers available for female students and children. You can specifically request a female teacher when booking your classes.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards, PayPal, bank transfers, and other local payment methods depending on your region. All payments are secure and processed through trusted payment gateways.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 7-day free trial so you can experience our teaching methodology before committing. If you're not satisfied after starting a paid course, please contact our support team to discuss refund options according to our refund policy.

Do you provide course materials and certificates?

Yes, we provide all necessary course materials including textbooks, worksheets, and supplementary resources. Upon successful completion of courses, students receive certificates that can be used for academic or professional purposes.

How are the classes conducted?

Classes are conducted via Zoom, Skype, or our proprietary online learning platform. Each session is one-on-one with video, audio, and screen sharing capabilities. The teacher uses digital whiteboards and other interactive tools to enhance the learning experience.

Can parents monitor their children's progress?

Absolutely. We provide regular progress reports to parents and encourage parental involvement. Parents can also request to observe classes (with prior notice) to see their child's progress firsthand.

What if I miss a class?

We understand that schedules can be unpredictable. If you need to miss a class, please provide at least 24 hours notice for rescheduling. We offer makeup classes within the same billing period for legitimate cancellations.

Do you offer group classes?

While our primary focus is one-on-one personalized instruction, we do offer small group classes (2-4 students) for certain courses like Islamic studies and Arabic language. Group classes are priced lower than individual sessions.

How do I register for a course?

Registration is simple! Fill out our contact form or message us on WhatsApp. Our team will get back to you within 24 hours to discuss your needs, assess your level, and schedule your free trial class. After the trial, you can choose to enroll in the course.
